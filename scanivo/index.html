This Privacy Policy describes how ScanDeck ("we", "our", "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application available on Google Play. By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: If you sign in, we collect your phone number or email used for OTP verification.
  • Documents and scans: Photos, PDFs, signatures, OCR text, tags, and folders you create are stored locally on your device. We do not upload them to our servers.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Camera & media access: The App accesses your camera and image gallery only when you actively scan a document or import a photo. Images are processed on-device.
  • Device & usage data: Anonymous crash logs, app version, OS version, device model, and basic usage events used to improve stability.
  • Advertising identifier (AAID): Used by Google AdMob to serve relevant ads (see Section 3).
  • Purchase data: Premium subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing. We receive only an entitlement status (active/inactive); we never see your card or payment details.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide core features: scanning, OCR, PDF creation, QR scan/generate, signing, watermarking, and the secure Locker.
  • To authenticate you via one-time password (OTP).
  • To deliver and measure ads (free version only).
  • To diagnose crashes and improve performance.
  • To process and validate Premium subscription entitlements.

3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following SDKs. Each operates under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob – serves banner ads and may use the Advertising ID. Policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Play Billing – processes Premium subscriptions. Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Services / ML Kit – used for on-device text recognition (OCR) and barcode scanning. Image data is processed locally and is not transmitted to Google.

4. Permissions We Request

  • Camera – capture document and ID card pages, scan QR codes.
  • Photos / Media (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES) – import existing images for PDF conversion.
  • Biometric – unlock the secure Locker using fingerprint or face.
  • Internet & Network State – serve ads, validate purchases, send OTP.
  • AD_ID – AdMob personalized/non-personalized ad delivery.
  • Billing – process Premium subscriptions through Google Play.

You may revoke any permission anytime via your device's Settings > Apps > ScanDeck > Permissions.

5. Data Storage & Security

All scanned documents, signatures, OCR results, and locked files are stored in the App's private storage on your device. Files placed in the Locker are protected by biometric / device authentication. We use industry-standard practices to safeguard data, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share data only with the third-party SDKs listed in Section 3, strictly for the purposes described, or when required by law.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR, CCPA, DPDP Act, etc.), you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising (Settings > Google > Ads > "Opt out of Ads Personalization").
  • Withdraw consent for data processing.
  • Request data portability.

To exercise these rights, email us at [your-email@example.com].

9. Data Retention

Local data (scans, PDFs, locker files) remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Anonymous analytics and crash data are retained for up to 24 months.

10. International Users

The App may be used worldwide. By using the App, you consent to the processing of information as described in this Policy.
