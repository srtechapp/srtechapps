Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 May 2026

Invonix ("we", "us", "our") provides an Android application for creating invoices, quotations, customer records, expenses, signatures and related business documents (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what data the App handles, how it handles it, and the choices you have. We've written it in plain language and kept it short.

Summary. Your business records (invoices, customers, signatures, etc.) live on your device. We do not collect or transmit them to our servers. Google Play, AdMob, and Google Play Billing receive their own limited data when you use those features — see Section 4.

1. Who we are

The App is published by the Invonix team. If you need to contact us for any privacy-related question, see Section 11 (Contact).

2. Data the App processes

The App processes the data you enter into it: business profile (name, phone, email, address, GSTIN), customers, invoices and quotation items, expenses, drawn or imported signatures, and template preferences.

Data Stored where Shared
Invoices, customers, expenses, business profile, signatures On-device Room (SQLite) database, plus exported files in Downloads/InvonixAI/ Only when you explicitly share, export, or back up a file
App preferences (theme, currency, defaults) On-device DataStore No
Subscription status Google Play Billing & on-device cache With Google Play (required to verify entitlement)
Crash diagnostics None collected by default No

3. How we use it

  • To render and export the invoices, quotations, and PDFs you create.
  • To save your business profile so it appears on every document.
  • To verify your Invonix Premium subscription via Google Play Billing.
  • To serve ads to non-premium users via Google AdMob.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing.

4. Third-party services

Google AdMob

Non-premium users see ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob collects the standard identifiers needed to deliver and measure ads, including the Android Advertising ID, approximate location derived from IP, device type and OS version. Personalised ads may be shown where consent permits; otherwise non-personalised ads are served. See Google's Ads & Privacy for detail. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads from your device settings.

Google Play Billing

Subscription purchases (Invonix Premium) are processed by Google Play. We do not see, store, or process your card details. Google's privacy policy applies to the billing flow.

Google Play Services

The App relies on Google Play Services for ads, billing, and Google sign-in (when you choose Google as your sign-in option for backup features). Google's privacy policy applies.

5. Permissions the App requests

The App requests only the permissions it needs. Where Android offers a runtime prompt, you can decline — the affected feature will be gracefully disabled.

  • Internet & Network state: required to load ads, contact Google Play Billing, and (optionally) reach the AI assistant features.
  • Billing: required to manage Invonix Premium subscriptions.
  • Storage (Android 9 and below only): required to write export files. On Android 10+ we use the scoped MediaStore API and need no storage permission.
  • Camera (optional): only requested if you tap "Take a photo" in the Signature screen. Photos are processed on-device.

6. How long we keep your data

On-device data is retained until you delete it from within the App or uninstall the App. Uninstalling removes the local database and all exported files we created in app-private directories. Files you saved into Downloads/InvonixAI/ stay in your device's Downloads folder — they're yours; we don't manage them after they're written.

7. Your rights

Because your business data stays on your device, you exercise the standard data rights directly:

  • Access & portability: use Backup & Restore to export a JSON snapshot of your data at any time.
  • Correction: edit any record from inside the App.
  • Deletion: delete records inside the App, or uninstall the App to remove the entire local database.

If you are in the EEA/UK and believe your rights have been violated, you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The App is a business tool and is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Security

Your business data is stored on your device using Android's standard application storage. We recommend keeping your device's OS up to date and using a screen lock. Backup files you generate are unencrypted JSON — handle them like you would any other business document.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App evolves. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be surfaced inside the App.

11. Contact

Questions or requests related to this policy can be sent to privacy@Invonix.ai. We aim to respond within 14 days.
